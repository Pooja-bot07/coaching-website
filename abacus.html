ABOUT ABACUS

Building stronger number skills.

Abacus learning introduces children to a structured way of working with numbers through guided practice and calculation activities.

The program is designed for children ages 4–14 and focuses on developing familiarity with numbers, concentration, memory, calculation speed and accuracy.

Through regular practice, students gradually become more comfortable working with calculations and build confidence in handling numbers.

WHAT STUDENTS CAN DEVELOP

Skills that go beyond the calculation.

Abacus learning combines calculation practice with activities that can help students develop useful learning skills.

Concentration

Practice activities encourage students to stay focused while working through calculations.

Memory

Regular practice can support the ability to remember and apply calculation techniques.

Calculation Speed

Students practise structured methods that can help them become quicker with calculations.

Accuracy

Repeated practice helps students work carefully and become more comfortable with calculations.

Mental Calculation

Learning activities introduce techniques for thinking through calculations with greater ease.

Confidence

Consistent practice can help students feel more comfortable and confident with numbers.

WHO CAN JOIN

A program for growing learners.

The Abacus program is suitable for children between 4 and 14 years. Learning activities can be adapted to the student's level and regular practice routine.

Ages 4–6

Early number familiarity, simple activities and guided practice.

Ages 7–9

Regular calculation practice with increasing familiarity and confidence.

Ages 10–14

Continued practice focused on calculation, speed, accuracy and confidence.
